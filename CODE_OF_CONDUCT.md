# Code of Conduct

deckforge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Briefly, we want everyone to take part without harassment. Be kind, welcome other people's points of view,
give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults,
discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

The rules apply in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/lrivallain). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may ban a contributor for a time or for good,
following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
